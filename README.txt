AROS GLOBAL RESOURCE — WEBSITE PACKAGE v2

- styles.css: responsive design

DEPLOY TO CLOUDFLARE PAGES:
1. Open Cloudflare Dashboard > Workers & Pages > your existing Pages project.
2. Choose Create a new deployment / upload new assets (wording may vary).
3. Upload this ZIP using Direct Upload / drag and drop, then Save and Deploy.
4. Check the production URL after deployment.

CONTENT NOTES:
- Product specs and packing details are intentionally presented as items to confirm for each order. They are not promises of stock, certification, grade, or fixed packaging.
- Legal standing text is cautious and does not publish NIB/NPWP numbers or imply permits/certificates not verified.
- Inquiry form opens a visitor's email app. It does not store submissions or send automatically from a server.
- Product/hero photography is loaded from Unsplash URLs and requires internet access.
- The logo image included is the logo file currently supplied in the project.
