Shared standards

Partnership & Responsibility

A successful supply chain needs more than a price. It needs defined roles, realistic capacity, accurate information, and agreed accountability.

  • Buyers: provide complete requirements and confirm acceptance criteria.
  • Suppliers: confirm capacity, origin, quality, lead time, and supporting documents.
  • Logistics partners: confirm route, equipment, handling, insurance where required, and delivery evidence.
  • AROS: coordinate communication and scope according to the written agreement.

Legal Standing

AROS GLOBAL RESOURCE operates through an Indonesian individual limited liability company structure. Company identity and business registrations can be shared with prospective counterparties during due diligence, subject to relevance and appropriate handling.

Product-specific permits, source legality, export registrations, certificates, and project qualifications are assessed according to the transaction. No permit or certification is implied unless specifically verified and documented.
